Schools & Departments

Swami Vivekananda University has faculty members across the following schools and departments (as listed on the official website).

  • School of Engineering – Computer Science & Engineering, Civil, Electrical, Electronics & Communication, Mechanical.
  • School of Humanities & Social Science – English, Journalism & Mass Communication, Education.
  • School of Agriculture – Agriculture.
  • School of Management – Management Studies.
  • School of Allied Health Science – Physiotherapy, Optometry, Food & Nutrition, Psychology, Medical Laboratory Technology, Medical Radiology & Imaging Technology.
  • School of Legal Studies – Legal Studies.
  • School of Computer Science – Computer Application, Data Science, Advanced Networking & Cyber Security, Multimedia & Animation.
  • School of Life Science – Biotechnology, Microbiology.
  • School of Basic Science – Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics.
